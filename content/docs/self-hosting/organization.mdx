---
title: Organization
description: Share recordings with everyone on your instance through one Organization folder tree.
---

A self-hosted Riffado can give its users a shared **Organization** next to
their private library. Owners share a recording by filing it into the
Organization tree; everyone on the instance can then open it, play it,
and generate transcripts and summaries for it. Nothing is copied: the
recording, its audio and the owner's private transcript and summary stay
exactly where they were.

## Turning it on

Set both variables and restart. On start, Riffado creates (or updates) one
**organization account** with these credentials.

```bash
SELF_HOST_MODE=shared             # the default
ORG_ACCOUNT_EMAIL=organization@example.com
ORG_ACCOUNT_PASSWORD=...          # 12+ characters, re-applied on every start
ORG_ACCOUNT_NAME=Acme             # optional, defaults to "Organization"
```

The email must not belong to an existing user; Riffado refuses to turn a
person's account into the organization account and logs why.

Removing the two variables makes the Organization **read-only**: what was
shared stays visible, nothing is deleted, and nothing new can be changed
except that owners can still withdraw their recordings. `SELF_HOST_MODE=local`
hides the Organization entirely.

Encryption at rest is unchanged: one instance key (`ENCRYPTION_KEY`) for all
data. The Organization is an access rule on top of it, not a separate
cryptographic domain.

## What people can do

| Action                                                  | Owner | Other users | Organization account |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | :---: | :---------: | :------------------: |
| Share a recording (file it in the Organization tree)    |  yes  |      -      |          -           |
| Withdraw a recording from the Organization              |  yes  |      -      |          -           |
| Create, rename, move, delete Organization folders       |  yes  |     yes     |         yes          |
| Move a shared recording between Organization folders    |  yes  |     yes     |         yes          |
| Open, play, download a shared recording                 |  yes  |     yes     |         yes          |
| Transcribe / summarize the Organization view            |  yes  |     yes     |         yes          |
| Name speakers in the Organization view                  |  yes  |     yes     |         yes          |
| Rename, merge or erase Organization people              |   -   |      -      |         yes          |
| Configure filesystem exports of Organization folders    |   -   |      -      |         yes          |
| Rename, delete, erase the recording itself              |  yes  |      -      |          -           |

Deleting an Organization folder never unshares anything: its recordings move
to the Organization root.

## Two views of one recording

- **Private view** (opened from *Private*): the owner's own transcript and
  summary, made with the owner's templates. Only the owner changes them.
- **Organization view** (opened from *Organization*): the organization's own
  transcript and summary. Until someone creates them it shows the owner's,
  read-only. Runs in this view use the clicker's AI provider, model and
  keys -- whoever clicks pays -- and the organization account's templates,
  prompts and output language. Title generation, Plaud title sync, webhooks
  and the owner's automations never fire from it.

Withdrawing or deleting a recording deletes its Organization view for
everyone at once.

## People

Speakers named in shared recordings become **Organization people**: one record
everyone names speakers with. Sharing a recording promotes every person
confirmed on it; a new name given in the Organization view is an Organization
person from the start. Suggestions and private notes are never shared --
notes stay visible only to whoever wrote them.

Everyone's People list shows their own people and the Organization's. Only the
organization account renames, merges or erases Organization people, and its
changes apply to every recording that names them, private ones included.
Anyone can fold a private duplicate into an Organization person.

## Retention

- Each owner's retention settings govern their own transcripts and summaries.
- The organization account's settings govern the Organization view's
  transcripts and summaries.
- Audio is shared, so while a recording is shared it is kept until **both**
  the owner's and the organization's audio periods have passed; without an
  organization audio period, shared audio is kept. After a recording is
  withdrawn, its owner's audio period applies again after a 7-day grace.
- Removing originals from Plaud is always the owner's setting alone.

## Exports

Sign in as the organization account, open an Organization folder and add a
filesystem export as usual. It writes the Organization view of every shared
recording in that subtree, with audio read from each owner's storage.
